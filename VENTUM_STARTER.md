## 2. Design mandate

The client supplied an industrial/electrical reference as a starting
point and explicitly expects Ventum to **do better**.

The final website must not be a clone of the client's reference or a
generic black/red factory template.

Target character:

-   industrial
-   technical
-   premium
-   modern
-   product-led
-   credible
-   visually authored
-   commercially believable
-   strong without becoming noisy
-   human rather than AI-looking

The working visual formula is:

**dark engineering atmosphere + editorial industrial composition + real
products + disciplined typography + controlled Ventum red/blue + light
editorial breaks + technical information + restrained motion.**

## 3. Logo rules

### Preserve the identity

Do **not redesign the Ventum logo**.

The logo work is restoration and production preparation:

-   remove blur/compression problems
-   improve edge quality
-   retain the existing geometry
-   retain the globe/lettermark identity
-   retain recognizable red/blue character
-   use high-resolution / transparent production assets
-   no fake chrome, glow, unrelated 3D, or decorative redesign

### Primary treatment

The preferred primary identity is the **large standalone
globe/lettermark Ventum mark**, rather than making the long text-heavy
lockup the default.

The supplied coloured logo treatment may also be used after clean
cropping/preparation.

Use the long horizontal lockup only where the layout genuinely benefits
from it.

### Production variants

Keep: - full colour - black - white - transparent versions - compact
mark - horizontal treatment where needed - small icon/favicon treatment

Approved restored assets are in `assets/brand/ventum_logo/`.

### Working blue family

-   Deeper blue/navy shades for depth

### Working red family

-   Ventum Red --- approximately `#D71920`
-   Highlight Red --- approximately `#F04444`
-   Dark crimson shades for depth

These are starter UI values. Final brand values should be reconciled
against the restored logo rather than replacing the logo colours.

### Gradient rules

Good: - navy → Ventum blue - deep blue → brighter blue - restrained blue
→ red - dark neutral with subtle red illumination - dark neutral with
subtle blue illumination

Avoid: - rainbow gradients - gradients on every surface - neon/cyberpunk
styling - excessive glow - gradient text everywhere

## 5. Typography

### Primary / display

**Manrope**

Use for hero copy, major headings, product names, model numbers,
important statistics and high-impact UI.

### Technical / UI

**IBM Plex Sans**

Use for body copy, specifications, metadata, filters, forms, tables,
catalogue UI, buttons and supporting navigation.

### Typography behavior

Prefer: - decisive scale contrast - large editorial headings - small
technical metadata - model numbers used graphically - disciplined
spacing - high readability

Avoid: - generic sci-fi fonts - too many font families - excessive
condensed typography - decorative type that weakens technical
credibility

The research direction moved toward: - premium dark

used selectively - strong product thumbnails - disciplined mobile
presentation

**MECUTO-type technical catalogue** - serious filters/specifications -
dark product detail presentation - technical information hierarchy -
catalogue UX should feel useful, not decorative

## 10. Homepage art direction

The homepage should not remain uniformly dark.

Recommended rhythm:

**dark cinematic hero → light/editorial product or service section →
dark technical section → light catalogue/product section → dark Brands
We Sell → editorial About → conversion/contact ending**

This creates visual pacing and makes Ventum feel more sophisticated than
a continuous black/red template.

### Hero

Target: - near-black/deep-navy foundation - strong red/blue atmospheric
accents - oversized Manrope headline - one real electrical/automation
product or carefully composed equipment visual - restrained navigation -
clear catalogue / WhatsApp CTA - minimal clutter - subtle motion, not
animation for its own sake

The sourced red "Engineering the Future" reference is an important
compositional influence, not a template to reproduce.

### Products

Product sections should mix: - editorial feature products - conventional
cards only where useful - large model names - manufacturer - price -
essential specs - genuine imagery - direct enquiry/action

Do not make the entire catalogue a repetitive grid.

### Brands We Sell

Use monochrome manufacturer marks on a clean architectural field. Motion
may be used if slow and controlled.

### About

The About section should use a **real photograph of the Ventum shop** as
the primary image.

Approved motion concept: - very slow cinematic pan/scale - almost
imperceptible movement - no aggressive parallax - preserve authenticity
of the shop - text remains stable and readable

The shop photograph is evidence of a real business and should not be
overprocessed into something artificial.

## 11. Interaction and motion

Motion should reinforce engineering precision.

Good: - slow image drift - controlled GSAP-style reveals - subtle
masking - smooth section transitions - restrained horizontal logo
movement - gentle product-image scale - purposeful hover states

Avoid: - bouncing - excessive springs - constant floating objects -
glowing cursor gimmicks - unnecessary 3D - animations that delay product
discovery - every section using a different motion language

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 16. Governing principle

**The client reference gives us permission to enter the industrial
visual territory. It does not define the quality ceiling.**

Ventum should combine the credibility of a real electrical supplier with
the visual confidence of premium industrial design.

When there is a conflict between "looking impressive" and preserving
factual product/brand integrity, **factual integrity wins**.

When there is a conflict between adding another visual effect and
maintaining restraint, **restraint wins**.

When a design decision feels like a generic template, **rework it**.
