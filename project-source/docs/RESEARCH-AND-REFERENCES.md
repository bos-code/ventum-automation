# Ventum — Research and Reference Direction

## Primary composition direction
Use industrial/electrical catalogue and automation websites as composition references, never as sources for fake Ventum products.

Reference set reviewed during the project:
- Prime Electric Wholesale Corp — warehouse-led hero and image-led category discovery.
- Creght/Brauer industrial manufacturing direction — dark hero, oversized circuit-breaker/product composition, minimal copy.
- VOLTIX Power Technology — dramatic breaker/panel product staging.
- Schneider Electric contactors/protection category pages — product-forward hierarchy.
- Siemens catalogue/product imagery — branded multi-product staging.
- DBS Automation website-design roundup — industrial automation composition patterns.
- Behance industrial automation/electrification and B2B industrial UI case studies.
- Zypsy / Podium Automation — premium industrial brand presentation.
- Industrial Site Design and Creative Vertical galleries.

## Working synthesis
Brauer-style composition + Voltix-like visual intensity + actual Ventum product photography.

References define layout, rhythm, hierarchy and atmosphere. Actual Ventum stock and exact verified manufacturer photography define product content. Never use a random Pinterest-looking product as final catalogue truth.

## ElectraFix direction
Use the supplied ElectraFix electrical-repair UI kit for clean responsive composition and spacing logic where useful. Do not blindly copy service-oriented content; adapt it to an image-first Ventum catalogue.

## Conversion model
1. Customer sees actual equipment quickly.
2. Customer can search/filter by system, category and brand.
3. Product page exposes image, exact model/price/specs.
4. Customer contacts Ventum by WhatsApp/enquiry with minimal friction.
